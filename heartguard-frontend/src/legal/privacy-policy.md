# HeartGuard™ Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** November 17, 2025

## 1. Introduction

HeartGuard™ ("we," "our," "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our Service.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide
- **Analysis Inputs:** Photos, chat messages, profile information you submit for analysis
- **Scammer Profiles:** Names, phone numbers, email addresses, social media handles, narratives
- **Account Information:** Email address, name (if you create an account)
- **Guardian Mode Contacts:** Names, emails, phone numbers of trusted contacts

### 2.2 Automatically Collected Information
- **Usage Data:** Pages viewed, features used, time spent, interactions
- **Device Information:** IP address, browser type, operating system, device identifiers
- **Cookies and Tracking:** We use cookies and similar technologies (see Section 8)
- **Log Data:** Access times, error logs, performance metrics

### 2.3 Information from Third Parties
- **AI Model Providers:** We may send data to AI service providers for analysis
- **Hosting Providers:** Vercel (frontend), Render (backend)
- **Analytics:** Usage analytics providers (if implemented)

## 3. How We Use Your Information

### 3.1 Primary Purposes
- **Provide the Service:** Generate Trust Scores, analyze conversations, create reports
- **Improve the Service:** Analyze usage patterns, improve AI models, fix bugs
- **Communication:** Send service updates, respond to inquiries
- **Safety:** Detect fraud, abuse, security threats
- **Legal Compliance:** Comply with legal obligations

### 3.2 Lawful Bases (GDPR)
- **Consent:** You provide explicit consent for analysis
- **Legitimate Interests:** Service improvement, fraud prevention
- **Legal Obligation:** Compliance with applicable laws

## 4. How We Share Your Information

### 4.1 Service Providers
We share data with:
- **Hosting Providers:** Vercel, Render
- **AI Providers:** OpenAI, Anthropic, or similar (for analysis)
- **Database Providers:** PostgreSQL hosting services

### 4.2 Legal Requirements
We may disclose information:
- To comply with legal process (subpoenas, court orders)
- To protect rights, property, or safety
- In connection with law enforcement requests
- To prevent fraud or security threats

### 4.3 Business Transfers
If we are acquired or merged, your information may be transferred to the successor entity.

### 4.4 With Your Consent
We may share information with your explicit consent.

### 4.5 We Do NOT
- Sell your personal information
- Share for third-party marketing
- Publicly disclose your analysis data

## 5. Data Retention

### 5.1 Retention Periods
- **Analysis Data:** Stored indefinitely unless you request deletion
- **Account Data:** Retained while your account is active
- **Logs:** Typically retained for 90 days
- **Backups:** May persist in backups for up to 1 year

### 5.2 Deletion
You may request deletion of your data (see Section 10).

## 6. Data Security

### 6.1 Security Measures
- Encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS)
- Encryption at rest for sensitive data
- Access controls and authentication
- Regular security audits
- Secure hosting infrastructure

### 6.2 No Guarantee
While we implement reasonable security measures, no system is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

### 6.3 Your Responsibility
- Use strong passwords
- Keep credentials confidential
- Report suspected security issues

## 7. Your Rights

### 7.1 General Rights
- **Access:** Request a copy of your data
- **Correction:** Request correction of inaccurate data
- **Deletion:** Request deletion of your data
- **Portability:** Request data in a portable format
- **Objection:** Object to certain processing

### 7.2 GDPR Rights (EU Users)
- Right to restriction of processing
- Right to withdraw consent
- Right to lodge a complaint with supervisory authority

### 7.3 CCPA Rights (California Users)
- Right to know what personal information is collected

- Right to non-discrimination
