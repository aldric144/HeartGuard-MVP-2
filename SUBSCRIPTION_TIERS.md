## 💳 Payment & Billing

### Accepted Payment Methods
- Credit/Debit Cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Discover)
- Apple Pay
- Google Pay
- PayPal (coming soon)

### Billing Cycle
- Monthly: Billed on the same day each month
- Annual: Billed once per year (save 20-25%)
- Prorated upgrades/downgrades

### Trial Period
- **7-day free trial** of Guardian Pro (no credit card required)
- **14-day trial** of Evidence Pro (credit card required, no charge until trial ends)
- Cancel anytime during trial with no charge

### Refund Policy
- **7-day money-back guarantee** on all paid plans
- No refunds if Evidence Locker™ PDFs were generated
- Prorated refunds for annual plans (within 30 days)

### Cancellation
- Cancel anytime from account settings
- Access continues until end of billing period
- Data retained for 30 days after cancellation
- Can reactivate within 30 days without data loss

## 🎯 Usage Limits & Enforcement

### Soft Limits (Warnings)
- At 80% of monthly limit: Warning notification
- At 90% of monthly limit: Upgrade prompt
- At 100% of monthly limit: Feature gated with upgrade modal

### Hard Limits (Blocks)
- Chat analyses: Blocked at monthly limit
- Photo verifications: Blocked at monthly limit
- PDF generation: Blocked at monthly limit (or pay-per-report)
- Guardian Mode: Disabled if over conversation limit

### Critical Safety Exception
- **Risk warnings ALWAYS shown** regardless of limits
- SafetyReply™ suggestions always available
- Scam Hotspot Map always accessible
- Basic trust score always calculated

### Limit Resets
- Monthly limits reset on subscription anniversary date
- Annual plans: Limits reset monthly (not annually)
- Unused limits do NOT roll over

---

## 🔄 Upgrade/Downgrade

### Upgrading
- Immediate access to new features
- Prorated billing (credit for unused time)
- Limits reset to new tier immediately
- Data retention extended automatically

### Downgrading
- Takes effect at end of current billing period
- Access to premium features until period ends
- Data retention adjusted at period end
- Excess data archived (not deleted)

### Plan Recommendations
- System suggests optimal plan based on usage patterns
- Notifications when consistently hitting limits
- Savings calculator shows annual vs monthly
